| D-021 | 2026-10-22 | **True contigs and the contig formula.** Two reads are linked if their true reference overlap is at least theta (here 30). A true contig is a chain of linked reads, counted from the known positions. For fixed read length, expected contigs = sum over start positions u of `(1 - m_u/S)^N - (1 - (m_u+1)/S)^N` with `m_u = min(l - theta, u)`, `S = G - l + 1` (derived in `genomeasm/coverage.py`). It equals brute-force enumeration exactly on a small case and agrees with 16 Monte Carlo comparisons.
